Why we built it

We built LegacyTrackr for ourselves.

We looked at every platform. The institutional ones assumed a staff we don't have: analysts, committees, a six-figure license. The portals only saw what the brokerage saw. And all of them belonged to someone else's business model.

We also realized we weren't unusual. In most first and second generation families, a handful of people make the decisions about investments, giving, and strategy. No staff, no committees, no reporting built for twelve stakeholders. The over-engineered institutional layer isn't something those families are missing; it is something they never needed.

We wanted a place to bring it all together. The accounts, the entities, the real estate, the private deals, the documents, and the instructions that go with them. A way to see complex wealth whole, and to share it with the people who need to know.

We wanted a platform we control, not a portal someone else gave us. We couldn't find it, so we built it.

What it does

The family office function, without the apparatus.

A family office keeps the whole picture: what exists, what it's worth, where the documents are, and what the plan is. That is the job LegacyTrackr does.

01

See it whole

Every holding in one private place: accounts, entities, real estate, private investments, operating businesses, digital assets, and the documents behind them. One clear picture, so decisions start from the whole balance sheet instead of a search through logins.

02

Keep it yours

You own the platform, not an advisor. You decide what goes in and who ever sees any of it, and you can keep it entirely to yourself. Nothing about your picture routes through anyone else's system or business model.

03

Share the plan

When you choose, bring in the people who need to understand it. A spouse with full access, children with a high-level view, an advisor with only what you grant. With instructions attached, so the plan can be carried forward, not reconstructed.

Your estate documents in one vault: will, trusts, powers of attorney, instructions.

A complete Personal Financial Statement, generated in seconds, whenever your attorney, banker, or CPA needs one.
